# Analytics

See how your videos perform on YouTube without leaving Reelpath. The data comes from your **real YouTube account**.

## Where to view metrics

| Where | What it shows |
|-------|---------------|
| **Home** | Summary across all your channels |
| **Channel → Analytics** | Data for a specific channel |
| **Video** (when opened) | Metrics for that published video |

## Refresh the data

- When you open **Home** or the **Analytics** tab, data updates automatically.
- Click **Refresh** if you want to update manually.

## Metrics you'll see

- Views
- Watch time
- Click-through rate on thumbnails
- Audience retention

> 💡 Recently published videos may take **24–48 hours** to show complete data on YouTube.

## Requirements

- Your channel must have **YouTube connected** (**Accounts** tab).
- The video must be **published** on YouTube (not just approved in Reelpath).

## If no data appears

1. Check that YouTube is **Connected**.
2. Click **Refresh** in Analytics.
3. Wait 24–48 hours if you just published.
4. If you still don't see metrics after reconnecting and waiting, [contact support](mailto:adripardo72@gmail.com) — enabling analytics access is Reelpath's responsibility, not yours.
