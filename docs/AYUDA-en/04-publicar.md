# Review and publish

Before uploading a video to YouTube, you can review it in Reelpath. If your channel has review enabled, you **must approve** each video.

## Review a video

1. Go to **Review** (you'll see a notice if videos are pending).
2. Open the video card and watch it in the player.
3. Check the title, duration, and visual quality.

## What you can do

| Button | What happens |
|--------|--------------|
| **Publish now** | The video uploads to your YouTube channel immediately |
| **Approve and schedule** | It publishes on the date you choose or the one the scheduler suggests |
| **Reject and delete** | The video is removed from Reelpath |

## Schedule publishing

When approving, you can choose:

1. **Use scheduler date** — Reelpath suggests the best time based on your calendar.
2. **Publish now** — immediate upload to YouTube.
3. **Choose date manually** — you pick the day and time (minimum 2 minutes in the future).

## Automatic Shorts

If you enabled Shorts on your channel, Reelpath creates vertical clips from the long-form video and publishes them on YouTube according to your settings.

Enable them on your channel → **Content** tab → **Shorts generation**.

## Upload a video you already have

If you recorded a video outside Reelpath and only want to generate Shorts:

1. On the channel detail page, find **Upload long video**.
2. Drag your MP4 file (maximum 500 MB, minimum 30 seconds).
3. Click **Upload and generate Shorts**.
