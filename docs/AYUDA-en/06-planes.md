# Plans and billing

Manage your subscription from **Settings → Plan**.

## Free trial

When you sign up, you get **14 days free** with:

- 1 channel
- 8 videos per month
- 2 generations per day

No credit card required to get started.

## Paid plans

| Plan | Price | Channels | Videos/month |
|------|-------|----------|--------------|
| **Creator** | €79/month | 1 | 8 |
| **Pro** | €149/month | 3 | 24 |
| **Studio** | €399/month | Unlimited | Unlimited |

Your account's exact limits appear in **Settings → Plan**.

## Subscribe or change plan

1. Go to **Settings → Plan**.
2. Click **Upgrade to this plan** on the card you want.
3. Complete payment with your card (secure payment via Stripe).
4. You'll return to Reelpath with the plan active.

For **invoices**, **update card**, or **cancel subscription**: click **Manage subscription**. This opens Stripe's billing portal.

## When the trial ends

If you don't subscribe to a plan, you won't be able to generate or publish videos. You'll see a notice in the dashboard. Choose a plan from **Settings → Plan** to continue.

## If you hit a limit

You'll see messages like:

- "You've reached the daily generation limit"
- "You've reached the monthly video limit"

Upgrade your plan or wait for the next period.
