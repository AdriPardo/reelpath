# Generate a video

Reelpath creates the video for you: it finds ideas, writes the script, generates images and voiceover, and assembles the final video ready to publish on YouTube.

## Start a generation

1. Open your channel from **Channels**.
2. Under **New generation**, enter a **Topic** (optional). If you leave it blank, Reelpath picks one based on your channel's niche.
3. Optionally, check **Schedule YouTube publish** and choose a date and time.
4. Click **Start generation**.

## Track progress

Go to **Generations** to see what stage your video is in:

- Finding ideas
- Writing the script
- Creating images and voiceover
- Assembling the video
- Waiting for your review (if enabled)
- Publishing to YouTube
- Creating Shorts (if enabled)

The screen shows how long it's been running. An 8–15 minute video usually takes **several minutes**.

## If something fails

1. Open the detail in **Generations**.
2. Read the error message.
3. Click **Retry** or **Resume** depending on what you see.

## Your plan limits

Your plan sets how many videos you can create per month and how many generations per day. If you hit a limit, you'll see a clear notice on screen.

Check your plan in **Settings → Plan**.
