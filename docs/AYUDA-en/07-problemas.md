# Troubleshooting

Quick answers to the most common issues in your production account.

## The video doesn't appear in Review

1. Check in **Generations** that the status isn't **Failed**.
2. Wait for the video assembly stage to finish.
3. Refresh the page.
4. If it failed, open the detail and click **Retry**.

## Can't connect YouTube

| What you see | What to do |
|--------------|------------|
| **Requires attention** | Click **Reconnect YouTube** in Accounts |
| Error signing in to Google | Use the Google account for the correct channel |
| Can't publish even though it says Connected | Reconnect and accept permissions again |

## Analytics are empty

1. Confirm that YouTube is **Connected**.
2. Check that the video is **published** on YouTube (not just approved).
3. Click **Refresh** in Analytics.
4. Wait 24–48 hours if you just published.
5. If there's still no data, [contact support](mailto:adripardo72@gmail.com).

## Generation failed

1. Open **Generations** and read the error message.
2. Click **Retry** or **Resume**.
3. Common causes: expired free trial, plan limit reached, or temporary service error.

## Can't generate or publish

Your trial may have expired or you may have hit a limit. Go to **Settings → Plan** and subscribe or upgrade.

## Need more help?

If the problem persists, [contact support](mailto:adripardo72@gmail.com) and include which channel, which video, and what error message you see.
