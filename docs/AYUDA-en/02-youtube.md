# Connect YouTube

To publish videos from Reelpath, you need to link your **real YouTube account**. Each Reelpath channel connects to **one** Google account.

## Steps to connect

1. Go to **Channels** and open your channel.
2. Open the **Accounts** tab.
3. Click **Connect YouTube**.
4. Sign in with the Google account for your YouTube channel.
5. Accept the permissions Google requests (upload videos and view analytics).
6. You'll return to Reelpath and see **Connected** with your channel name.

> 💡 Reelpath does not store your Google password. It only keeps the authorization until you revoke it.

## Who can see your videos

On the **Accounts** tab, you choose the default visibility for new videos:

- **Public** — anyone can watch them
- **Unlisted** — only people with the link
- **Private** — only you

You can change this anytime.

## If you see "Requires attention"

This means the connection expired or you revoked permissions in Google.

1. Open your channel → **Accounts** tab.
2. Click **Reconnect YouTube**.
3. Sign in again and accept the permissions.

## Disconnect

Click **Disconnect YouTube** on the **Accounts** tab. Videos you've already published on YouTube **are not deleted**.

## Common issues

| What you see | What to do |
|--------------|------------|
| **Requires attention** | Click **Reconnect YouTube** |
| Can't publish | Check that you're connected and using the correct Google account |
| Analytics are empty | Reconnect YouTube, click **Refresh** in Analytics, and wait 1–2 days after publishing. If there's still no data, [contact support](mailto:adripardo72@gmail.com) |

> ⚠️ If you see a message about internal platform configuration, you don't need to do anything in Google Cloud: email [support](mailto:adripardo72@gmail.com) and we'll look into it.
