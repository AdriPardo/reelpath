# Team and invitations

You can work with other people on the same Reelpath account.

## Invite someone

1. Go to **Settings → Team**.
2. Enter the person's **email**.
3. Choose their role: **Member** or **Administrator**.
4. Click send invitation.

- If they already have a Reelpath account, they're added instantly.
- If they're new, they receive an email with a link valid for **7 days**.

## Roles

| Role | What they can do |
|------|------------------|
| **Owner** | Everything, including billing |
| **Administrator** | Manage team, plan, and settings |
| **Member** | Create videos, review, and publish |

Only the **Owner** and **Administrators** can change the plan and billing.

## Remove a member

In **Settings → Team**, find the person and remove them from the list.
