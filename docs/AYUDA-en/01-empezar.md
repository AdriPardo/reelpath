# Getting started with Reelpath

Reelpath helps you create YouTube videos with artificial intelligence: you choose the topic, review the result, and publish it to your real channel.

## Create your account

1. Click **Sign up** and enter your **email** and a **password** (minimum 6 characters).
2. Click the button to create your account.
3. You'll land in the dashboard right away — no email confirmation needed.

You get a **14-day free trial** with full access.

## Sign in to the dashboard

1. Click **Sign in**.
2. Enter your email and password.
3. Click **Sign in to dashboard**.

## Your first three steps

On **Home**, you'll see a task list. Complete them in this order:

1. **Create your first channel** — go to **Channels**, add a name and your content topic (for example: "historical curiosities").
2. **Connect YouTube** — open your channel, go to the **Accounts** tab, and click **Connect YouTube**.
3. **Generate your first video** — on the channel detail page, click **Start generation**.

When you finish all three, the list disappears.

## Where to find everything

| Menu | What it's for |
|------|---------------|
| **Home** | Overview and quick links |
| **Generations** | Videos currently being created |
| **Review** | Videos ready for your approval |
| **Videos** | All your videos |
| **Channels** | Configure each channel |
| **Settings** | Your account and plan |
| **Help** | These guides |
